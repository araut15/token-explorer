# Token Explorer

An interactive explorer from my **Payments, Decoded** series on Medium.

Who sees your real card number? Add a card to a phone wallet, tap to pay, try to steal the token, and lose your phone. At every step, four panels show exactly what your phone, the store, the card network and your bank can see.

**Try it:** open the live page from this repository's GitHub Pages link.

**Read the article:** *Tap to Pay: How Digital Wallets and Tokens Protect Your Card* on Medium.

Example numbers only (4111 1111 1111 1111 is a standard test card). Real systems are more involved.
